Payment structure
During the recovery period, each device's monthly fee is its hardware price ÷ recovery months, plus its hosting fee. After recovery, monthly fee is hosting fee only. Both hardware price and hosting fee are taken from Computed pricing above. Annual in advance is that year's actual total revenue under this structure, paid as one lump sum at the start of the year instead of monthly, it correctly reflects a mixed recovery/standard year if the recovery period doesn't land on a 12-month boundary.

Loan term defaults to the recovery period for two-phase, or 12 months for Hybrid, when you select that structure, edit freely afterward. The advance lands in month 1; repayment starts month 2 and runs for the loan term (so the last instalment falls in month, loan term + 1). Both repayment methods charge interest on the outstanding balance, not the original principal, straight-line keeps principal constant and lets the payment fall over time, amortisation keeps the total payment constant and lets the principal/interest split shift. Only the interest portion counts as a cost in Annual P&L and the profitability metrics, the principal repayment does not, since the hardware cost was already recognised once, in month 1.
