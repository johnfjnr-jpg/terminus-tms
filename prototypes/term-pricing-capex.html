Tax on these invoices

GST per invoice line.
Split WHT: hardware rate on CAPEX payments up to the hardware value (1,550,000.00); any CAPEX above that, and all subscription, at the SaaS rate. Single WHT applies to the whole invoice.
Not carried over from Commercials: installation, annual invoicing, PO factoring.

Rules on this screen

1. Base TCV sets approval, margin and the margin floor.
2. Deal value is Base TCV unless CPI is locked, then Final TCV.
3. CAPEX amount defaults to hardware; Custom overrides it.
4. Subscription = (Base TCV − CAPEX) / term; CPI applies to it only.
5. Rounding cents go on the last instalment, last milestone and last month.
