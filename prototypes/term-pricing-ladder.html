Term Pricing · price ladder mockup v2

TP_LADDER, for John's approval (L-13). v2 after John's 17/11 rulings: the formula is gone; every product prices from its price ladder (list price); sales adjusts per deal by a discount % or a deal fee, either-or; the compare card retires; each product's price ladder editor carries a price chart and a profit chart.
Figures are illustrative, worked by hand. Seeds: SafeSight as approved; Air Quality as ruled (option a: SafeSight step-downs from 1,000); HEMIR 15,000 at 12 months, stepping down by SafeSight's percentages. Costs used: SafeSight 8,000 + 200/mo, Air Quality 2,000 + 50/mo, HEMIR 100,000 + 200/mo. The build reads them from the catalog.
Frames A and B: 1 SafeSight, 1 Air Quality, a 5% deal discount, no volume band reached.
